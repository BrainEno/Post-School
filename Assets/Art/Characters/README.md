# Character art

Use graybox placeholders until the owner approves a visual direction and asset provenance. Character identity should remain readable at gameplay distance.

The prototype and visual-calibration builder now use two original, procedural student placeholders composed from Unity primitives in `Assets/Scripts/Character/ProceduralStudentModelFactory.cs`. They are broad visual experiments informed by owner-provided references only; no Drive image, model, texture, name, or exact design was imported or copied. They are not final or production-cleared character art.
